The History of Hogwarts

Hogwarts was founded more than one thousand years ago by four great wizards: Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw and Salazar Slytherin.

They built this castle together to teach magic to young people who had the gift. Each founder created one house, carrying their own values and beliefs.

For centuries, Hogwarts has remained the most famous magic school in the wizarding world.

Gryffindor

Founded by Godric Gryffindor. Its symbol is the lion, with house colours scarlet and gold. Gryffindor values courage, bravery, nerve and chivalry. Those who stand up for what is right often belong here. Famous members include Harry Potter, Hermione Granger and Albus Dumbledore.

Hufflepuff

Founded by Helga Hufflepuff. Its symbol is the badger, with house colours yellow and black. Hufflepuff treasures loyalty, patience, fairness and hard work. Hufflepuff students are sincere and unafraid of toil. Newt Scamander and Cedric Diggory are well-known Hufflepuffs.

Ravenclaw

Founded by Rowena Ravenclaw. Its symbol is the eagle, with house colours blue and bronze. Ravenclaw respects wisdom, wit, curiosity and learning. Students here love thinking and exploring knowledge. Luna Lovegood is one of its most famous members.

Slytherin

Founded by Salazar Slytherin. Its symbol is the serpent, with house colours emerald green and silver. Slytherin values ambition, cunning, leadership and resourcefulness. Many Slytherins are clever and determined to achieve their goals. Severus Snape and Draco Malfoy are famous Slytherins.
