Famous Figures of Hogwarts

Albus Percival Wulfric Brian Dumbledore

Albus Dumbledore served as the Headmaster of Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry for decades. Widely regarded as the greatest wizard of modern times, he was also the Supreme Mugwump of the International Confederation of Wizards and Chief Warlock of the Wizengamot. He was wise, kind, and deeply committed to protecting the wizarding world from the Dark Arts. He discovered the twelve uses of dragon's blood, worked alongside Nicolas Flamel, and was the only wizard Lord Voldemort ever truly feared. Beyond his power, he was known for his calm wisdom, love of lemon drops and knitting patterns, and his unshakable belief in the power of love and courage.

Harry James Potter

Known as "The Boy Who Lived", Harry Potter is the only person known to have survived the Killing Curse. Orphaned as an infant when Lord Voldemort murdered his parents, he was raised by his Muggle relatives, the Dursleys, unaware of his magical heritage until his eleventh birthday. Upon arriving at Hogwarts, he was sorted into Gryffindor House, where he became best friends with Ron Weasley and Hermione Granger. He became the youngest Seeker in over a century to join a house Quidditch team. Throughout his school years, he repeatedly faced Voldemort and his followers, ultimately defeating the Dark Lord and ending the Second Wizarding War. He is defined by his bravery, loyalty, and innate sense of justice.

Hermione Jean Granger

A Muggle-born witch sorted into Gryffindor House, Hermione Granger is one of the most brilliant students in Hogwarts history. Gifted with extraordinary intelligence and an unmatched work ethic, she consistently achieved top marks in every subject and often helped her friends keep up with their studies. Far from being just a bookworm, she showed immense courage and resourcefulness throughout her years at Hogwarts, using her knowledge of spells, potions and magical lore to get Harry and Ron out of countless dangerous situations. She co-founded Dumbledore's Army, fought in the Battle of Hogwarts, and later went on to become Minister for Magic, advocating for the rights of Muggle-borns and magical creatures.

Ronald Bilius Weasley

Ron Weasley is Harry Potter's first and closest friend at Hogwarts. Born into the large, warm-hearted Weasley family, a pure-blood household known for its loyalty and humble lifestyle, he was sorted into Gryffindor House alongside Harry and Hermione. Though he often felt overshadowed by his five older brothers and his famous best friend, Ron repeatedly proved his bravery and loyalty. He was a talented chess wizard, a skilled Keeper on the Gryffindor Quidditch team, and a steadfast companion through every dangerous adventure. His sense of humor, common sense and unwavering friendship were indispensable in the fight against Voldemort, and he fought bravely alongside his friends in the Battle of Hogwarts.

Severus Snape

Severus Snape was the Potions Master and later Head of Slytherin House at Hogwarts, also serving briefly as Defence Against the Dark Arts professor and eventually Headmaster. A master of potions and the Dark Arts, he was known for his sharp, sarcastic demeanor and his apparent hostility toward Harry Potter. For many years he appeared to be a loyal follower of Lord Voldemort, but it was later revealed that he had been working as a double agent for Dumbledore for nearly two decades, driven by his lifelong love for Lily Evans, Harry's mother. His complex character embodies courage, remorse and quiet sacrifice. He is remembered as one of the bravest wizards in the history of the wizarding world.

Minerva McGonagall

Professor Minerva McGonagall was the Transfiguration teacher at Hogwarts, Head of Gryffindor House, and later Deputy Headmistress and Headmistress of the school. A highly skilled witch and a registered Animagus who can transform into a tabby cat, she was known for her strict discipline, sharp wit, and deep sense of fairness. Though stern in class, she cared deeply for her students and repeatedly defended them from danger. She was a member of the Order of the Phoenix, fought valiantly in both Wizarding Wars, and led the defence of Hogwarts during the final battle. She is remembered as one of the most respected and capable educators in Hogwarts' history.

Rubeus Hagrid

Rubeus Hagrid was the Keeper of Keys and Grounds at Hogwarts, and later the Care of Magical Creatures professor. A half-giant with a warm, gentle nature, he was the first person to tell Harry Potter about his magical heritage and deliver his Hogwarts letter. Though expelled from Hogwarts in his third year, he remained on the grounds as a staff member and became one of Harry's most loyal friends. He had an enormous love for all magical creatures — even dangerous ones — and owned a variety of exotic pets throughout the years, including his boarhound Fang, his pet dragon Norbert, and his giant spider Aragog. He was a member of the Order of the Phoenix and fought bravely in the Battle of Hogwarts.

Lord Voldemort (Tom Marvolo Riddle)

Born Tom Marvolo Riddle, Lord Voldemort was the most powerful Dark wizard of all time and the central antagonist of the wizarding world. A half-blood wizard orphaned at birth, he was raised in a Muggle orphanage until Dumbledore invited him to attend Hogwarts, where he was sorted into Slytherin House. Obsessed with blood purity, immortality and power, he created seven Horcruxes to cheat death, splitting his soul into multiple fragments. He gathered followers known as Death Eaters and waged two wars against the wizarding community, spreading terror across Britain. His reign of terror ended when he was finally defeated by Harry Potter at the Battle of Hogwarts, bringing peace back to the magical world.
